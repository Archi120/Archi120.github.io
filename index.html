I’m curious about how machine learning works, where it falls short, and what we can do to make it more reliable.

At Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology, I’ve worked on classifying ovarian cancer tissue images and studying how machine learning systems respond to attacks. I also build web and Android applications, including a platform for elderly users.
