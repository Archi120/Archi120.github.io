ICCIT 2025

01

Impact of Stain Normalization and Background Filtering on Deep Learning & Transformer-based Models for Ovarian Cancer Histopathology Classification

For this study, I compared ways of preparing ovarian cancer tissue images before feeding them into deep learning models. Using the UBC-OCEAN dataset, I found that filtering out the background improved classification across the model architectures we tested.

02

Reinforcement Learning-Driven Adversarial Evasion Attack on Machine Learning-Based Network Intrusion Detection Systems

For my undergraduate thesis, I used reinforcement learning to create attacks that try to evade network intrusion detectors. I developed PPO- and A2C-based attacks, then tested how they transferred across five types of machine learning models using CIC-IDS2017 and LycoS-UNICAS-IDS2018.
